Once your custom Identity or E.G.O is finished, the [forum](/forum) is where you share it, see what others have made and trade feedback. This guide covers everything from logging in to writing a description that gets people talking.

## Log in

Posting, commenting and cloud saves need an account. Click **Login** in the header and sign in with Google. Your Google profile picture and name become your avatar and display name, and your account page lists everything you've posted.

## Make a cloud save of each card

Posts are built from your cloud saves. In the [Identity Creator](/creator/identity) or [E.G.O Creator](/creator/ego):

1. Open **Setting/Saves** in the footer.
2. Switch to **Cloud saves**.
3. Click **Create a new save** and give it a name you'll recognize, because you'll search for it by name when posting.

Local saves only exist in your browser, so they can't be attached to a post. See [Saving your work](/blog/card-creator-guide#saving-your-work) for how the save types differ.

## Create the post

Click **Post** in the header (or **Create new Post** on the forum) and fill in the form:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Post name | Required, up to 199 characters. Something descriptive, like "Loan Shark Rodion (000 Bleed Identity)", helps people find it. |
| Tags | Up to 20. Add the Sinner and the main status effects so people can find your post with the forum filters. The **Identity** or **Ego** tag is added automatically based on the cards you attach. |
| Cards | Choose **Identity** or **Ego**, then search your cloud saves by name and pick one. You can attach 1 to 8 cards and mix both types, for example an Identity with its matching E.G.O. |
| Description | Optional, but it's what turns an image into a conversation. See the next section. |

## Write a good description

A card on its own shows the numbers. The description tells readers what you were going for. A few sentences on each of these go a long way:

- **The concept:** who is this Identity or E.G.O, and where in the City do they come from?
- **How it plays:** the main status effect, how the skills work together, and what the passives reward.
- **Inspiration:** the story, song or official Identity that gave you the idea.
- **Credits:** the artist for any art that isn't yours.
- **What you want feedback on:** balance, wording, art placement, or anything else.

The description editor supports bold, italics, links and lists, so you can break longer write-ups into sections.

## After posting

Your post appears at the top of the forum and on the home page. Other players can view it, open the cards full size and leave comments. Reply to feedback in the comments, and browse the forum by Sinner or status effect to find similar creations and return the favor.
