Once a custom Identity or E.G.O is finished, the [forum](/forum) is where you share it, see what others have made and trade feedback. This guide walks through the whole flow: preparing your saves, filling in the post form, what readers see on your post, writing a description worth commenting on, and keeping the post up to date after you change the card.

## Log in

Posting, commenting and cloud saves need an account. Click **Login** in the header and sign in with Google. Your Google name and profile picture become your display name and avatar, and your profile page lists everything you've posted.

## Before you post: cloud saves

A post is built from your cloud saves, not from whatever is open in the editor. For each card you want in the post:

1. Open the card in the [Identity Creator](/creator/identity) or [E.G.O Creator](/creator/ego).
2. Open **Setting/Saves** in the footer and switch to **Cloud saves**.
3. Click **Create a new save**, or **Overwrite** an existing save if you've been working on one.

Give each save a name you'll recognize, because the post form finds saves by searching their names. Something like "Rodion - Bleed 000 v3" is easier to pick out than "Save 2026-11-21".

Local saves only exist in your browser, so they can't be attached to a post. See [Saving your work](/blog/card-creator-guide#saving-your-work) for how the save types differ.

### "Your save is still uploading"

When you create or overwrite a cloud save, its images (the preview, splash art, icons and skill images) finish uploading in the background. If you try to post that save in the first few seconds, the post form shows "Your save is still uploading, try again in a few seconds." Nothing is wrong. Wait a moment and press the button again.

## Create the post

Click **Post** in the header, or **Create new Post** on the forum, and fill in the form.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Post name | Required, up to 199 characters. **It can't be changed after posting**, so check it before you submit. Including the Sinner and the main mechanic, like "Rodion - Bleed Loan Shark (000)", helps people find it. |
| Tags | Up to 20. Add the Sinner and the main status effects so the post shows up in the forum's tag filters. The **Identity** and **Ego** tags are added automatically from the cards you attach. |
| Cards | Choose **Identity** or **Ego**, search your cloud saves by name and pick one. Repeat for each card. You can attach 1 to 8 cards and mix both kinds, for example an Identity with the E.G.O it pairs with. The same save can't be added twice, and the cards appear on the post in the order you added them. The **X** on a card removes it. |
| Description | Optional, but it's what gets people talking. The editor has bold, italic, underline, and bulleted and numbered lists. See [Write a description that gets feedback](#write-a-description-that-gets-feedback). |

Press **Post**. You're taken to the new post as soon as it's created.

## What readers see

When you post, the site takes a copy of each attached card. The post page shows every card's image, and next to it a **card summary** with the card's header, its stats, and tabs for **Skills**, **Passives** and **Effects**. Arrows switch between cards when the post has more than one.

- Readers can click any of your custom status effects in the summary to open a popup with its icon and description, the same popup you see in the editor.
- They can select and copy skill text, and status effect names come along as plain text, so quoting your card in a comment or on Discord works.
- The copy is fixed at the moment you post. Changing or overwriting the save afterwards doesn't change the post until you update that card yourself (see [Editing your post](#editing-your-post)).

Posts with no card details and a very short description are kept out of search engines. Posts made through cloud saves always carry their card details, but a good description still makes your post easier to find and far more likely to get comments.

## Write a description that gets feedback

The card summary already shows every number. The description is where you say what the numbers are for. Readers know the game, so skip explaining how statuses work and talk about your design: what it's trying to do, how its pieces fit together, and what you're unsure about.

Starting each paragraph with a bold label keeps a longer write-up easy to scan. These labels cover most designs:

- **Concept.** Who this version of the Sinner is, which part of the City they're from, and what the visual idea is.
- **How it plays.** The core loop in a few sentences: what builds the main resource, what spends it, and what the passives reward.
- **Team fit.** Which existing Identities or E.G.Os it works with, and which statuses or resonance it wants.
- **Credits.** Who made the splash art, if it isn't yours.
- **Questions.** The one or two things you want feedback on. Specific questions get specific answers.

### An example

Here is a description for a made-up Identity, to show how the parts fit together.

> **Concept.** Hong Lu as a Zwei Section 2 instructor who teaches by example. The splash art is a commission from a friend, credited below.
>
> **How it plays.** Skill 1 builds Protection on himself. Skill 2 converts his Protection into Protection for the ally with the lowest HP, then attacks. Skill 3 hits harder the more Protection he gave away this turn. The passive gives a small Clash Power bonus to allies standing next to him, so he wants to be in the middle of the lineup.
>
> **Team fit.** He's built for teams with fragile damage dealers. I tested the numbers against the current Bleed and Rupture teams, and he slots into both without needing their resources.
>
> **Credits.** Splash art by a friend, used with permission.
>
> **Questions.** Is the Skill 3 scaling too strong when two Protection sources stack? And does "lowest HP" read clearly on Skill 2, or should it say "lowest HP percentage"?

The example spends no words on what Protection or Clash Power do. Its effort goes into intent and two precise questions, which is what reviewers can actually answer.

## Editing your post

Open your post and click **Edit** next to the share button. The button only appears for the post's author, and only while you're logged in.

The edit page is the post form with your current post filled in.

- **The title is locked.** It's shown greyed out and can't be changed.
- **Tags and description** can be changed freely. The Identity and Ego tags are recalculated from the cards you end up with.
- **Each card** has its own buttons:
  - **Update** takes a fresh copy from the card's cloud save. Use it after you overwrite the save in the editor. The card is marked "Will update" until you save the post.
  - **Replace** swaps the card for a different cloud save. Press it, then search for and pick the new save. Press **Cancel** to back out.
  - **Undo** puts back the card the post had before you pressed Update or Replace.
  - **X** removes the card. It's disabled when only one card is left, because a post needs at least one.
- If a card's cloud save has been deleted, the card shows "Save deleted" and has no Update button. It keeps displaying fine, and you can still replace or remove it.

Press **Save changes**. The post updates straight away, and the post page shows an "Edited" date next to the posted date.

If saving fails with "This ID/EGO is no longer part of the post", the post was changed in another tab. Reload the edit page and make your changes again.

## Deleting your post

Click **Delete** next to **Edit** on your post. A confirmation asks you to check, because a deleted post is hidden from everyone and you can't bring it back yourself. After deleting, you're taken to your profile page. The cloud saves that were in the post aren't touched.

## Keeping saves and posts in sync

The cloud save menu in the editor shows a collapsible **Posted in** list under every save you've used in a post, with a link to each post. After changing a save, open that list to find which posts need an **Update**.

Deleting a cloud save that is used in posts warns you how many posts use it. Those posts keep their copy of the card, but they lose the link to the save, so the card can no longer be updated from it. If you only want a fresh start, overwrite the save instead of deleting it.

## After posting

New posts appear at the top of the forum and on the home page. Readers can comment, open the share menu to copy a link or post it to X, Reddit or Facebook, and browse to your profile for your other posts.

- **Reply in the comments.** Answering questions and saying what you changed keeps the thread going.
- **Update instead of reposting.** If feedback leads to a new version, overwrite the save, then use **Update** on the post. The comments stay with the post, and readers can see it was edited.
- **Return the favor.** Use the forum's tag filters to find designs for the same Sinner or status effect, and leave the kind of feedback you'd like to get.

For a reference of every keyword you can use in skill text, see [Keywords and Status Effects](/blog/keywords-and-status-effects).
